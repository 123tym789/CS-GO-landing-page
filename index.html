About CS:GO

Born from the Counter-Strike series, Global Offensive elevated the tactical FPS genre to worldwide esports dominance. Two teams. One objective. Pure skill decides the winner.

Counter-Strike: Global Offensive (CS:GO) is a multiplayer first-person shooter developed by Valve and Hidden Path Entertainment. Released in 2012, it continues the iconic Counter-Strike legacy with refined mechanics, competitive matchmaking, and a thriving esports scene featuring multi-million dollar tournaments.

Players join either the Terrorists or Counter-Terrorists in objective-based rounds. Whether planting or defusing the bomb, or securing hostages, every decision counts. The economy system, weapon spray patterns, and map knowledge create a skill ceiling that rewards dedication and teamwork.

5v5 Competitive
Global Matchmaking
Esports Ready

The heart of CS:GO. Ranked 5v5 matches on official maps with strict rules: 30-round format (MR15), overtime if tied, and skill-based matchmaking. Climb from Silver to Global Elite and prove your worth on the leaderboard. Requires perfect teamwork, communication, and individual aim.

Money is won by kills, objectives, and round wins. Teams must manage their economy carefully — force-buying, eco rounds, and full buys dictate strategy. A lost pistol round can cascade into a series of disadvantaged rounds. Master the economy and you control the pace of the game.

The classic mode. Terrorists plant the C4 at one of two bombsites. Counter-Terrorists must prevent the plant or defuse the bomb once planted. 1:55 plant timer, 40-second defuse (or 10 with kit). Site executes, mid control, and rotates define high-level play on maps like Dust II and Mirage.

Every weapon has unique recoil patterns, movement inaccuracy, and first-shot accuracy. Learning spray control for the AK-47 and M4, mastering the AWP's one-shot potential, and understanding peeker's advantage separates average players from the pros. Practice in aim maps and deathmatch.

The most iconic map in Counter-Strike history. Long A, catwalk, B tunnels, and mid doors. Simple layout, endless depth. Perfect for learning fundamentals and executing classic strategies.
